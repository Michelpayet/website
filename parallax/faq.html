FAQs

Why Seychelles?

Visit Seychelles for its stunning beaches, vibrant Creole culture, and rich history. Experience a blend of African, European, and Asian influences in its music, dance, and cuisine. Explore colonial architecture, traditional festivals, and folklore. With crystal-clear waters and diverse wildlife, Seychelles offers a unique mix of natural beauty and cultural heritage.

When is the best time to visit?

The best time to visit Seychelles is from April to May and October to November. During these months, the weather is warm and dry, ideal for beach activities and exploring the islands. These transitional periods between the monsoon seasons offer pleasant temperatures and lower humidity.

Which island to visit?

In Seychelles, each island offers a unique experience:
Mahé: The largest island, home to the capital Victoria, stunning beaches, lively markets, and great hiking trails.
Praslin: Famous for the UNESCO-listed Vallée de Mai nature reserve and beautiful Anse Lazio beach.
La Digue: Known for its laid-back vibe, traditional oxcarts, and the iconic Anse Source d'Argent beach.

Is Seychelles expensive?

Seychelles can be expensive, but it offers options for various budgets. While luxury resorts and fine dining are available, you can also find affordable guesthouses, local eateries, and self-catering apartments. With careful planning, you can enjoy this beautiful destination regardless of your budget, exploring its stunning beaches and rich culture.

What laguages are spoken in Seychelles?

In Seychelles, the main languages are Seychellois Creole (a French-based Creole), English, and French. Seychellois Creole is the most widely spoken language, used in daily life. English is commonly used in government, business, and education, while French also holds cultural and historical importance.
